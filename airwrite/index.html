HOW IT WORKS

Your hand becomes the interface.

Allow webcam access, hold your hand inside the writing zone, and extend your index finger. AIRWRITE tracks your hand landmarks in the browser and renders a smooth virtual-ink trail.

01Allow camera
02Extend index
03Write in air

AIRWRITE now includes browser-side OCR for clear block-style letters, words, names and common symbols. For best results, write large, separated characters inside the writing zone and press Recognize.
